By 
Jonathan Munshaw



            Friday, June 25, 2021 10:37
        


                                Features
                            

                                Talos Takes
                            




By Jon Munshaw.With major cyber attacks in recent years against major U.S. critical infrastructure suppliers like Norsk Hydro and Colonial Pipeline, we’re in a new world of CI cybersecurity. New threats require new approaches to defense. And in the U.S., this is likely going to include partnerships between those who manage critical infrastructure, government and the private cybersecurity sector.Talos recently outlined what this may look like in America. One of the authors of that post, Joe Marshall joins the show this week to talk about public-private partnerships to defend critical infrastructure.
