Talos Takes back with new episode, feed


By 
Jonathan Munshaw



            Monday, February 3, 2020 11:34
        


                            Talos Takes
                        




Talos Takes, our new bite-size podcast, is back with its own feed and a new show.We first unveiled Talos Takes in early December, and took some time to develop a new Talos Podcasts page to accommodate Talos Takes and Beers with Talos. Now you have two Talos shows you can subscribe to!We'll be adding Talos Takes to Apple Podcasts, Google Play and other services very soon.
