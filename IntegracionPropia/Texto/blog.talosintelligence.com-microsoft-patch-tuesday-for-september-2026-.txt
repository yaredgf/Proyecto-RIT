By 
Cisco Talos

                            Patch Tuesday
                        




Microsoft has released its monthly security update for September 2026, which includes 973 vulnerabilities affecting a range of products, including 113 that Microsoft marked as "critical."Microsoft notes that 2 of the vulnerabilities disclosed this month have been exploited in the wild:CVE-2026-81963 affects Windows Update Stack.
