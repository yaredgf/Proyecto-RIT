Beers with Talos Ep. #78: Fingerprints and hunting parties


By 
Mitch Neff



            Tuesday, April 21, 2020 17:30
        


                            Beers with Talos
                        




If iTunes and Google Play aren't your thing, click here.  Recorded April 10, 2020  We have a couple great topics today — and only one of them is a COVID-19 related topic. So, it turns out that you can fake fingerprints. The good news is that it takes a lot of time, equipment and expertise. It is much easier for a criminal to just make you unlock it yourself. We have also seen an unprecedented level of collaboration and righteous anger across all vendors responding to COVID-based scams. Literally everyone is just watching, documenting, and hunting anyone screwing around with anything even tangentially related to the current health crisis.
