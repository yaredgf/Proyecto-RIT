OT Threat Research

The OT Threat Research Lab uses industrial systems and connected technologies to examine how emerging threats can affect operations. Its findings help security and operations teams detect risk earlier, prioritize action and protect the systems their organizations depend on.

As AI, automation, and connected technologies become part of critical infrastructure, the security challenges become more complex. A threat that once affected data can now disrupt physical operations. Understanding those risks before they become problems is what drives our work. The research we publish helps organizations better understand and defend the systems they depend on.

Why OT Threat Research, Why Now

Cyber threats no longer stop at the network. As digital and operational environments become more connected, organizations need a clearer understanding of how emerging threats can affect operations. OT Threat Research provides practical insight to strengthen defenses before risk becomes disruption.

OT Threat Research Lab Capabilities

The OT Threat Research Lab provides a dedicated environment for testing emerging technologies, evaluating AI-OT scenarios, and examining how threats can move across connected operational systems. Its findings inform vulnerability analysis and technical guidance that help organizations strengthen detection, prioritize risk, and protect resilient OT operations.
